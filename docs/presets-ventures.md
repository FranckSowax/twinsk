# Presets cinématiques par venture — Seedance 2.0

Prompts complets, prêts à injecter dans `Higgsfield:generate_video` (`params.model: 'seedance_2_0'`, `params.prompt: <prompt ci-dessous>`).
Les prompts modèle sont **en anglais** (Seedance les interprète mieux). Le contexte et les réglages sont en français.
Adapter les crochets `[…]`, drafter en basse résolution, puis verrouiller en HD.

> **Timelapse de transformation = OPTIONNEL.** Les prompts principaux ci-dessous sont des plans cinématiques propres, SANS transformation du décor. N'ajouter la couche « ⚙️ Option timelapse » que si Franck la demande explicitement.

## 1. OhMyGab — marketplace e-commerce

**Concept** : plan produit chaleureux d'un smartphone montrant la marketplace, entouré de produits, avec une validation de paiement Mobile Money à l'écran. Énergie mobile-first, sans transformation du décor.
**Aspect ratio** : `9:16` (WhatsApp Status / Reels / TikTok). **Durée** : 6–8 s. **Ambiance** : vibrant, premium, mobile-first.

**Prompt principal**
```
Slow cinematic push-in on a smartphone resting on a warm wooden table in a sunlit Libreville home, the screen glowing with a clean mobile marketplace app interface in green and gold. Around the phone, a tidy arrangement of vibrant products — fresh fruit, folded fabric, a small electronic device, a sealed delivery parcel — sits in soft focus. On the screen, a Mobile Money payment confirmation animates with a gentle green pulse. Equatorial golden-hour light, shallow depth of field, premium product-commercial look, subtle ambient motion (steam, light flicker). Motion: smooth slow push-in at normal speed. Mood: vibrant, premium, effortless. --no text, watermarks, logos, distorted faces, extra limbs
```

**Variante "main + WhatsApp"** (commande conversationnelle)
```
Static locked-off macro shot of a hand holding a smartphone showing a green WhatsApp-style chat where a shopping order is being placed, a Mobile Money confirmation glowing softly on screen. A neatly packed parcel rests on the table beside the phone in soft focus. Warm domestic Libreville interior, soft window light, cinematic shallow focus, gentle natural hand motion. Motion: locked-off with subtle real-time movement. Mood: friendly, modern, trustworthy. --no text, watermarks, logos, distorted faces, extra limbs
```

**⚙️ Option timelapse (sur demande uniquement)** — remplacer le mouvement par : *« The camera stays perfectly fixed. In a smooth time-lapse, the products materialize and build around the phone from an empty table into an abundant arrangement. »* et passer Motion à `fluid time-lapse`.

## 2. DriveBy Africa — import de véhicules

**Concept** : hero shot automobile d'un véhicule premium importé, lumière qui balaye la carrosserie. Révélation cinématique par le mouvement caméra, sans matérialisation.
**Aspect ratio** : `16:9` (site web / YouTube) ou `9:16` (Reels). **Durée** : 8–10 s. **Ambiance** : powerful, premium, aspirational.

**Prompt principal**
```
Slow cinematic orbit around a premium imported SUV parked in a polished showroom at golden hour, warm light sweeping across its gleaming bodywork and chrome details. Reflections glide over the paint as the camera arcs smoothly. Volumetric god rays through tall windows, reflective floor, high-end automotive commercial aesthetic, deep contrast. Motion: fluid slow orbit at normal speed, settling into a held beauty frame. Mood: powerful, premium, aspirational. --no text, watermarks, logos, distorted faces, people
```

**Variante "low-angle hero"**
```
Static locked-off low-angle shot of a premium imported vehicle on a quay at first light, a misty West African port softly blurred in the background. Warm sunrise light rakes across the bodywork, fine reflections shifting, faint heat haze rising. Cinematic, epic scale, anamorphic-style flares, shallow depth of field. Motion: locked-off with subtle light and atmospheric movement. Mood: epic, reliable, premium. --no text, watermarks, logos, distorted faces, people
```

**⚙️ Option timelapse (sur demande uniquement)** — remplacer le mouvement par : *« The camera never moves. In a cinematic time-lapse the vehicle materializes at center frame — first as light and reflections, then fully solid — as sunrise light sweeps across it and dust settles into stillness. »* et passer Motion à `dramatic slow time-lapse`.

```
Slow cinematic push-in on a focused young Gabonese student studying at a desk in a warm Libreville room, books and a laptop in front, soft golden light from a nearby window. The student looks up with quiet ambition toward the light. Behind, a softly out-of-focus poster of a modern Chinese university campus is just visible. Warm hopeful light, cinematic depth, inspirational tone, gentle natural movement. Motion: smooth slow push-in at normal speed. Mood: hopeful, aspirational, focused. --no text, watermarks, logos, distorted faces, extra limbs
```

```
Static locked-off macro shot of an open passport and a university acceptance document resting on a desk, soft warm key light, a steaming cup of tea beside them in soft focus. Subtle dust motes drift in the light beam. Premium, clean, optimistic, shallow depth of field. Motion: locked-off with subtle ambient motion. Mood: opportunity, pride, momentum. --no text, watermarks, logos, distorted faces, people
```

## 4. KardAfrica — cartes cadeaux digitales

**Concept** : beauty shot fintech d'une carte cadeau premium qui flotte et tourne lentement sur fond noir réfléchissant, shimmer holographique. Haut de gamme, sans effet d'assemblage.
**Aspect ratio** : `9:16` (social / app) ou `1:1` (carrousel). **Durée** : 5–6 s. **Ambiance** : premium, digital, gifting.

**Prompt principal**
```
Slow elegant rotation of a sleek matte-black gift card floating just above a dark reflective surface, lit by soft cyan and gold rim light. The card turns gently, its premium metallic finish catching a subtle holographic shimmer, a soft glow pulsing outward to suggest value and reward. Elegant fintech product-commercial aesthetic, deep blacks, crisp specular highlights, shallow depth of field. Motion: smooth slow rotation and gentle float at normal speed. Mood: premium, digital, generous. --no text, watermarks, logos, distorted faces, people
```

**Variante "moment de don"** (usage cadeau)
```
Static locked-off shot of two hands in warm light, one passing a glowing digital gift card toward the other against a soft dark gradient background. As the card changes hands, a faint golden light bloom radiates and settles. Premium, emotional, minimal, shallow depth of field. Motion: slow elegant slow-motion. Mood: warm, premium, celebratory. --no text, watermarks, logos, distorted faces
```

**⚙️ Option timelapse / assemblage (sur demande uniquement)** — remplacer le mouvement par : *« The camera never moves. In a fluid motion the card assembles itself from drifting particles of light, settling into a premium metallic finish. »* et passer Motion à `smooth particle-assembly resolving into a slow float`.

## 5. Gabarit générique Sowax Group

Pour toute autre venture (Notarius, Kartelle.io, Twinsk, Gabon Insight, Sowax Group corporate). Remplir les crochets. Plan cinématique propre par défaut.

```
[Slow push-in / slow orbit / static locked-off] shot of [sujet central de la marque] in [décor / contexte CEMAC], lit by [golden hour / studio key light / neon noir]. [Détail vivant dans le cadre : reflets, lumière, poussière, mouvement naturel subtil]. [Élément invariant marché si pertinent : Mobile Money screen / WhatsApp chat / FCFA value / OHADA seal]. [Aesthetic : premium product-commercial / sober institutional / vibrant marketplace], cinematic depth, [warm / high-contrast] grade, shallow depth of field. Motion: [smooth slow movement / locked-off with subtle ambient motion] at normal speed. Mood: [2–3 mots]. --no text, watermarks, logos, distorted faces[, people si aucun humain]
```

**⚙️ Pour ajouter un timelapse (sur demande)** : remplacer le mouvement par *« The camera stays fixed. In a fluid time-lapse, [transformation : vide→plein / chantier→bâti / abstrait→concret] unfolds around the subject. »* et passer Motion à `fluid time-lapse`.
